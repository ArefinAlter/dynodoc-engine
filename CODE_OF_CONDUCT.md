# Code of conduct

This project welcomes people of every background and experience level. Treat
participants with respect, give constructive technical feedback, accept correction,
and keep disagreements focused on ideas and behavior.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate
intimidation and sustained disruption are unacceptable. These standards apply in
project repositories, issues, pull requests and other official project spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at arefinalter@gmail.com. Include links
or other context when safe to do so. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.
Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and avoid unnecessary disclosure. A
maintainer involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome alone.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, request a correction, issue a warning,
restrict participation temporarily or permanently, or report credible threats to
the relevant platform. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its
impact. Retaliation against someone making a good-faith report is prohibited.
A participant may request reconsideration with additional context through the same
private channel. Project maintainers are accountable to these standards as well.
